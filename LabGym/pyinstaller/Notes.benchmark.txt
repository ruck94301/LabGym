To estimate a duration on a platform with Geekbench 6 value GB6, 
multiply the reference duration, T_ref, by GB6_ref/GB6.

For example, if an operation duration is reported as
    5.1 min (@GB6=1889)
or,
    T_ref = 5.1 min (@GB6_ref=1889),
that is, on a MacBook Air with 
    (a) 2.2 GHz Dual-Core Intel Core i7, 
    (b) 8GB RAM, 
(having a Geekbench 6 of ~1889),

Then an estimate of the duration on a 
"MacBook Air (13-inch, 2025) with M4, Multi-Core" with GB6 of ~14830
is
    0.65 min = 5.1 min * (1889/14830)

The choice of Geekbench 6 as a basis benchmark comes from a ChatGPT 
recommendation 2025-09-03.
